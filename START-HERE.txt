SELF-IMPROVING CLAUDE: START HERE

These skills are for Claude Code (the terminal app or the Claude desktop app's Code tab).
They read files on your own computer, so they do NOT work as Claude.ai skill uploads.

Install:
  Copy everything in the "skills" folder into ~/.claude/skills/
  (On a Mac: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G, type ~/.claude/skills, and drag the 2 folders in.)

Then, in Claude Code, say:
  "Improve system"
Claude reads your saved sessions and shows you a report. Pick what you want built.

Full instructions are in README.md.
